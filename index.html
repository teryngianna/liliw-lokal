    Origin

    More than
    a destination

    Founded in 1571, Liliw is known for its cultural heritage, traditional craftsmanship, and scenic surroundings near Mount Banahaw. Today, its local economy continues to thrive through the livelihoods of its people, including small shops, eateries, and service providers. Through Liliw Lokal, local MSMEs are given greater visibility to showcase their products, services, and stories, encouraging more people to discover and support the community.
