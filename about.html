    About us

    Behind
    the project

    We are Bachelor of Multimedia Arts students specializing in Graphic Design. Through our capstone project, we developed Liliw Lokal, a digital platform that features selected MSMEs through improved promotional materials, creative visuals, and accessible business information.

    Mission

    Promote

    To help selected MSMEs present their businesses more effectively through creative promotional materials and an accessible digital platform that highlights their products, services, and unique identities.

    Vision

    Reach

    To create a space where emerging businesses gain greater visibility, reach wider audiences, and have more opportunities to grow through thoughtful design and digital promotion.

    Frequently asked questions

    Have questions? Learn more about our platform, its features, and how it works.

    Liliw Lokal is a creative initiative that uses a web-based platform to promote Liliw’s local industries and businesses through improved promotional materials, business information, and stories. It aims to make local offerings easier to discover, support, and appreciate.

    Liliw Lokal currently features selected local MSMEs under three industries: Food, Crafts, and Hospitality. More local industries may also be featured in the future to further showcase Liliw’s diverse business community.

    Visit the Discover page and choose a category to browse its featured businesses. You can view each business’s profile to learn more about its offerings and background.

    Each business page provides relevant details, including its location, operating hours, contact information, and external links. You may reach out to the business directly for inquiries or visits.

    Availability, prices, and services may change over time. For the latest updates, we recommend contacting the business directly.

    Interested business owners may submit their business information through the Get Featured section. Simply follow the provided instructions to send your application for review.

    Businesses must operate within Liliw, be registered or recognized by the municipality, and belong to one of the industries covered by Liliw Lokal.

    No. Getting featured on Liliw Lokal is free of charge. The initiative aims to help more people discover and appreciate Liliw’s local businesses.
